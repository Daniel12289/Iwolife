ABOUT

IWO LIFE V1.1 is an original life-sim made for the browser. The city is inspired by Iwo / Osogbo, Osun State, Nigeria. Multiplayer chat, wallet, services and reporting use Firebase. Voice calls use WebRTC.

Desktop: WASD move, SHIFT sprint, SPACE jump, E interact, F vehicle, P phone, R camera, ESC menu.
Mobile: joystick, ACTION / JUMP / SPRINT / CAR / 📱 buttons.
